FSA-LM3's question bank avoided a repeat of this issue by assigning `correctChoiceIndex` programmatically via round-robin at generation time rather than relying on manual rotation discipline; see KI-L17's sibling note in `question-audit.json` under `.local-research/fsa-verification/FSA-LM3/`.
