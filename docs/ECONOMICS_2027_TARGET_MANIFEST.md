Every future lesson must cover its mapped outcomes, use original prose and graphics, include independently recalculated examples, and pass module verification before publication.

**ECO-LM5 delivery note (v1.7.5):** delivered under the planned id `economics-05-introduction-to-geopolitics` shown above, with 40 official questions/flashcards (target: 40), 4 original graphic elements meeting the 4-graph target (1 original 2x2 archetype matrix table, 1 original tools-to-quadrants comparison table, and 2 interactive SVG visualizations embedded in the module's single tool), and 0 formulas against a mapping-stage target of 2 (source research confirmed the official 52-page reading contains no explicit symbolic formula anywhere — a purely conceptual/framework-based module — so no formula was invented; see `.local-research/economics-verification/ECO-LM5/formula-audit.json`). Source research confirmed no exhibit-numbering shift and no substantive content difference between the 2026 and 2027 editions, the mildest edition-to-edition delta of any Economics module produced to date. This module covers a politically sensitive subject; a secondary provider (Zell Education) was found to introduce unsourced current-events political framing absent from the official text, and this was explicitly excluded from all lesson, question, and flashcard content per the project's neutrality requirement.

**ECO-LM4 delivery note (v1.7.4):** delivered under the planned id `economics-04-monetary-policy` shown above, with 45 official questions/flashcards (target: 45), 7 original graphic elements meeting the 7-graph target, and 2 formulas against a mapping-stage target of 10 (source research confirmed the official reading contains exactly one explicit symbolic formula — the neutral policy rate — in the entire 36-page module; money-multiplier, Fisher-relationship, Taylor-rule, and quantity-theory-of-money content appear only in secondary providers, not the official 2027 text, so no additional formulas were invented; see `.local-research/economics-verification/ECO-LM4/formula-audit.json`). Source research confirmed no exhibit-numbering shift between 2026 and 2027 for this module (unlike ECO-LM3), with two small genuine factual updates (France's supervisor renamed to ACPR; FOMC mechanics updated to the modern administered-rates description).

**ECO-LM3 delivery note (v1.7.3):** delivered under the planned id `economics-03-fiscal-policy` shown above, with 40 official questions/flashcards (target: 40), 5 original graphic elements meeting the 5-graph target, and 7 formulas against a mapping-stage target of 8 (source research confirmed the official reading gives no symbolic formula for debt-ratio dynamics, structural-balance calculation, or crowding-out magnitude, so no 8th formula was invented; see `.local-research/economics-verification/ECO-LM3/formula-audit.json`). Source research confirmed ECO-LM3's body text and worked examples are identical to the 2026 curriculum, aside from an exhibit-numbering shift (2027 numbers a previously unnumbered opening exhibit, shifting all subsequent exhibit numbers by one relative to 2026).

**ECO-LM2 delivery note (v1.7.2):** delivered under the planned id `economics-02-understanding-business-cycles` shown above, with 40 official questions/flashcards (target: 40), 4 formulas (target: 4), and 8 original graphic elements addressing the 8-graph target (2 ASCII decision-flow diagrams, 3 original comparison tables, and 3 SVG visualizations embedded in the module's 2 interactive tools) rather than 8 standalone chart images; see `.local-research/economics-verification/ECO-LM2/graph-audit.json` for the reconciliation. Source research confirmed ECO-LM2 is a word-for-word zero-change carryover from the 2026 curriculum.

**ECO-LM1 delivery note (v1.7.1):** the planned id `economics-01-the-firm-and-market-structures` above was proposed during the v1.7.0 mapping-only release, before any routes or public assets existed under any id. Production found that the repository's existing preliminary lesson `economics-firm-market-01` already covered these five official outcomes and was fully wired through the manifest, search index, formula/flashcard/practice pages, and chapter exam. Per the project's standing rule to preserve stable content IDs and routes absent an explicit migration need, v1.7.1 expanded that existing lesson to the full 45/45/12/3 production targets in place rather than introducing a new lesson under the planned id. `economics-01-the-firm-and-market-structures` is retained here only as the historical planning record.
