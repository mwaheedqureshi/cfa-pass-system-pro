**ECO-LM8 delivery note (v1.7.8):** delivered under the planned id `economics-08-exchange-rate-calculations` shown above, with 35 official questions/flashcards (target: 35), 4 original graphic elements meeting the 4-graph target (1 quote-convention schematic, 1 direct-multiplication-vs-inversion diagram, 1 original recreation of the official Exhibit 1 sample forward-points table, and 1 arbitrage/no-arbitrage-cycle diagram), and 8 formulas matching the 8-formula mapping-stage target exactly (source research confirmed the official 20-page reading — PDF pp. 259-278, of which pp. 275-278 are the book-wide Economics glossary rather than module content — states exactly 8 formula families: direct-multiplication and inversion cross-rate methods, forward-points-to-rate, forward premium/discount percentage, the arbitrage relationship, forward-rate-from-arbitrage, forward-as-expected-spot, and the day-count-adjusted forward; see `.local-research/economics-verification/ECO-LM8/formula-audit.json`). This is the only Economics module produced to date requiring no downward revision of either its formula or graph mapping-stage target. Source research confirmed zero substantive content difference between the 2026 and 2027 editions, and confirmed ECO-LM8 preserves ECO-LM7's identical A/B (price/base) currency-quotation convention without restatement, adding only a generic f/d (foreign/domestic) relabeling for the arbitrage-relationship derivation. Independent recalculation of every worked example caught and corrected an inverted hedged-return arbitrage formula before release (see `KNOWN_ISSUES.md` KI-L09). This is the final official Economics module; all 8 of 8 are now produced. Economics-wide certification and content-freeze were completed in v1.7.9.

**ECO-LM7 delivery note (v1.7.7):** delivered under the planned id `economics-07-capital-flows-and-the-fx-market` shown above, with 45 official questions/flashcards (target: 45), 6 original graphic elements against an 8-graph target (source research confirmed the official 38-page reading contains exactly 6 exhibits, all data tables with zero charts or diagrams; 3 elements recreate official Exhibit 2-6 content and 3 are original pedagogical additions, including a quote-convention schematic and the tool's regime-spectrum SVG), and 5 formulas against a 7-formula mapping-stage estimate (source research confirmed the official reading states 4-5 distinct symbolic formula families — the real exchange rate level, its percentage-change approximation, the base-currency percentage-appreciation formula with an explicit non-symmetry caveat, and the trade balance identity — with no cross-rate, interest-rate-parity, or forward-rate formula, since that content is confirmed deferred to the separate, later ECO-LM8 module; a 5th formula honestly formalizes the source's own stated reciprocal-quote relationship; see `.local-research/economics-verification/ECO-LM7/formula-audit.json`). This module is unusually vulnerable to currency-quotation-direction errors; the lesson opens with a dedicated, explicit A/B (price/base) notation-convention section, and every calculation, question, and the interactive tool state this convention explicitly.
