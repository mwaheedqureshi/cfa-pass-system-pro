# v1.6.2 — Quantitative Methods 2027 curriculum alignment

This release maps Quantitative Methods to 11 official learning modules while retaining 12 stable study routes. Official LM7, Estimation and Hypothesis Testing, is presented as two study lessons and counts complete only when both are complete.

The existing `quant-distributions-07` route now teaches **The Return and Risk of a Financial Portfolio**. Its original distribution bank was moved to canonical LM6, `quant-probability-06`; the new portfolio bank contains 45 original questions, 45 flashcards, 10 formula records, and a 30-question chapter exam through the existing engine.

Progress storage migrates once to version 2. It transfers old distribution completion and notes to LM6, preserves original notes under a legacy key, removes the semantically obsolete bookmark, resets portfolio completion, and retains historical practice records with legacy metadata. Unrelated preferences and progress remain unchanged.

All Quantitative data records carry study-lesson, official-module, subdivision where applicable, and supplementary classification. Default comprehensive assessment selection excludes supplementary records and groups by official module.

Remaining mathematical verification work is recorded honestly in the private remediation ledger. Automated checks establish structural consistency but do not replace an independent line-by-line review of every legacy example.
