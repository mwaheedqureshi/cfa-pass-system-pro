# Financial Statement Analysis assessment and flashcard plan

No questions or flashcards are generated in v1.8.0. Future targets reflect source page length, LOS count, calculation intensity, statement-analysis/exhibit intensity, and (once researched during each module's own production release) local mock coverage, following the same planning method used for Economics in v1.7.0.

General question-mix principle for FSA (adapted from the Economics mix, reweighted toward FSA's heavier calculation and exhibit-analysis emphasis): definitions/classification 10%, calculations 25% where supported, statement/exhibit analysis 20% where supported, ratio or accounting-treatment interpretation 25%, multi-step/scenario analysis 10%, integrated/mock-style challenges 10%; reallocate any unsupported category to interpretation, consistent with the Economics precedent of never forcing an unsupported category. Exactly three choices per future question, balanced answer positions, and complete explanations for every option, per `DEVELOPMENT_STANDARDS.md`.

## FSA-LM1 — Introduction to Financial Statement Analysis

- Questions: 35; low calculation intensity, so this module leans toward definitions, framework-step sequencing, and information-source classification rather than numeric calculation; the 25% calculation-mix slot reallocates to interpretation and scenario application.
- Flashcards: 35; balance framework steps, regulatory filing types, information sources, audit report opinions, and reporting-system contrasts.

## FSA-LM2 — Analyzing Income Statements

- Questions: 50; the topic's most calculation-heavy "individual statement" module — heavy weight on basic/diluted EPS (treasury-stock and if-converted methods, antidilution tests), revenue/expense recognition classification, and common-size income-statement ratios.
- Flashcards: 50; balance revenue/expense recognition rules, capitalize-vs-expense classification, EPS formulas and worked patterns, non-recurring item treatment, and common exam traps (e.g., antidilutive securities, weighted-average share timing).

## FSA-LM3 — Analyzing Balance Sheets

- Questions: 40; balance disclosure-interpretation questions (intangibles, goodwill, financial instruments, non-current liabilities) with common-size balance-sheet calculation and ratio interpretation.
- Flashcards: 40; balance disclosure rules, classification tests, common-size ratio definitions, and comparisons across intangible-asset origin (purchased/developed/acquired).

## FSA-LM4 — Analyzing Statements of Cash Flows I

- Questions: 45; emphasize direct/indirect method preparation and conversion mechanics, statement-linkage reasoning, and IFRS/US GAAP classification contrasts.
- Flashcards: 45; balance direct vs. indirect method definitions, conversion formulas, linkage relationships, and IFRS/US GAAP classification differences (interest, dividends, taxes paid/received).

## FSA-LM5 — Analyzing Statements of Cash Flows II

- Questions: 35; emphasize FCFF/FCFE calculation from multiple starting points and cash-flow-ratio interpretation; shorter module, proportionally smaller target.
- Flashcards: 35; balance FCFF/FCFE derivation paths, cash flow performance/coverage ratio definitions, and common-size cash flow interpretation.

## FSA-LM6 — Analysis of Inventories

- Questions: 40; heavy calculation weight (FIFO/LIFO/weighted-average COGS and ending-inventory mechanics, inflation/deflation ratio effects, LIFO reserve adjustments) balanced with disclosure-interpretation items.
- Flashcards: 40; balance cost-flow-method definitions, inflation/deflation effect rules, LIFO reserve adjustment mechanics, and lower-of-cost-and-NRV rules.

## FSA-LM7 — Analysis of Long-Term Assets

- Questions: 35; balance intangible-asset reporting-treatment classification, impairment/derecognition calculation, and disclosure interpretation.
- Flashcards: 35; balance intangible-asset origin treatment, impairment test steps, derecognition mechanics, and disclosure-interpretation traps.

## FSA-LM8 — Topics in Long-Term Liabilities and Equity

- Questions: 45; balanced across its three sub-topics (leases, pension/stock-based compensation, and long-term liability/equity disclosures) given the module's broader LOS scope.
- Flashcards: 45; balance lease classification/measurement rules, defined-benefit funded-status and periodic-cost mechanics, stock-based compensation expense recognition, and disclosure requirements.

## FSA-LM9 — Analysis of Income Taxes

- Questions: 40; heavy calculation weight (deferred tax asset/liability creation, effective/statutory/cash tax rate reconciliation) balanced with disclosure interpretation.
- Flashcards: 40; balance temporary-vs-permanent-difference classification, deferred tax mechanics, tax-rate definitions, and reconciliation-disclosure interpretation.

## FSA-LM10 — Financial Reporting Quality

- Questions: 45; the topic's most conceptual module (quality spectrum, red-flag detection, earnings-management methods) — the 25% calculation-mix slot reallocates almost entirely to interpretation and scenario/case-study analysis, consistent with the honest reallocation principle.
- Flashcards: 45; balance quality-spectrum vocabulary, conservative-vs-aggressive definitions, earnings-management motivation and method classification, and warning-sign checklists.

## FSA-LM11 — Financial Analysis Techniques

- Questions: 55; the topic's largest target, reflecting its role as the core ratio-formula and synthesis module — heavy weight on activity/liquidity/solvency/profitability ratio calculation, DuPont decomposition, and multi-step scenario analysis combining ratios across statements.
- Flashcards: 55; balance every ratio category's formula and interpretation, DuPont 3-factor and 5-factor components, ratio-relationship reasoning, and industry-specific ratio applications.

## FSA-LM12 — Introduction to Financial Statement Modeling

- Questions: 40; balance pro forma model construction/calculation, behavioral-bias identification, and forecast-horizon/terminal-value reasoning.
- Flashcards: 40; balance pro forma construction steps, behavioral bias definitions and remedies, Porter's five forces concepts, and forecast-horizon considerations.

## Totals

505 official questions and 505 official flashcards planned across all 12 modules (see `docs/FSA_2027_TARGET_MANIFEST.md` for the per-module table). These are planning-stage targets only; actual production counts are subject to the same honest, source-verified reconciliation discipline already applied throughout Economics (never forced to hit a target, never invented).
